What the app shows you.

Five screens. Every number on them comes from your own devices.

Activities

Every session from Strava or Apple Health, with time in range and the glucose line on the card. Runs, rides, swims, walks, ski days.

Plan

Pick the activity and duration, set where your glucose is and which way it's heading. The app finds the sessions that matched and shows what happened.

Insulin

Daily totals from Glooko or your pump, basal and bolus split, insulin on board when you plan.

Connections

Dexcom, Nightscout, Apple Health, Strava, Glooko, Tandem t:slim. Connect once, then it's automatic.
