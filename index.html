The founder

Built by an athlete who gets it

I've lived with Type 1 Diabetes for over 30 years. We're told that exercise is absolutely critical for managing this condition but there's no instruction manual. For most of us, it's pure trial and error. What makes it harder is that it's a deeply personal condition, and what affects one person doesn't always translate to another, making it hard to learn from anyone but yourself.

But over time, patterns emerge. A data-driven approach lets you see those patterns faster, iterate quicker, and stop guessing. That's why I built GlucoseInsights. Whether you're lacing up for your first 5K or your 10th ultramarathon, our tools can help you get there.
